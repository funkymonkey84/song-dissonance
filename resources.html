Glossary

Terms used on this site
TermMeaning
Affective dissonanceA clash between the emotion in a song's music and the emotion in its words.
PositivenessA 0 to 100 score of how happy a song sounds, apparently Spotify's “valence.”
Emotion labelA category (joy, sadness, anger, fear, love, surprise) assigned to a song's lyrics by a language model.
MismatchA song with Positiveness of 67 or higher and a sadness, anger, or fear label.
CappedLimited to 25 songs per artist per decade so no artist dominates.
